# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): the middle number
moves for new behaviour, the last for fixes alone. The newest version goes at
the top; a release is refused if its version has no section here.

## 0.3.0

- Various bug fixes.

## 0.2.1

- **Fixes:** ore veins, herbs and chests are found on every language client, and ooze-covered veins, obsidian and more crates are known too.
- **Fixes:** the beam shows over you when no spot of a nearby node is known.
- **Fixes:** account sharing takes one-word names.

## 0.2.0

- **Fishing buttons:** a small bar with Cast and Add Lure shows while a fishing pole is in your hand.
- **Node Pointer:** an arrow points you to herbs, ore and chests close to you, and a beam of light or sparkles marks the spot. Pick the arrow, the beam or both.
- **Node Sound:** a ping or a spoken line plays when the pointer finds a node.
- **Fixes:** the beam and sparkles now show on Era when the game gives no nameplate.

## 0.1.0

First release for WoW Classic Era.

- **Find your farm:** search any material, mob or zone to see where it drops, the zone and the levels.
- **Rankings and Grind:** mobs sorted by what one kill is worth, and the best camps for cloth, leather, ore, herbs or fish at your level.
- **Gather to cap:** the Ore, Herbs and Leather views show your way from your skill to 300.
- **Kills and Loot panel:** your kills, your loot and gold an hour. Drop rates come from your own kills.
- **Goals:** set a goal like 40 Copper Ore or a number of kills. An arrow points you to the nearest place to get it.
- **Map:** your herb, ore and fishing spots show on the map.
- **Handy extras:** an Open button for clams and sacks, a trash can for your cheapest item when your bags are full, and a Stats tab with charts.
- **Languages:** English, Deutsch (German), French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
